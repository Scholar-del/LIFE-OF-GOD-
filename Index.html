Bible Reading Challenges

Pick a pace that fits your life and read through the whole Bible on your own schedule.

Steady pace

1-Year Challenge

About 3–4 chapters a day

Reads through the entire Bible over 365 days, mixing Old and New Testament each week so no single book overwhelms your week.

Focused pace

6-Month Challenge

About 6–7 chapters a day

Covers the whole Bible in half the time, best for anyone who already has a daily reading habit and wants to go deeper faster.

Intensive pace

3-Month Challenge

About 13–14 chapters a day

A focused sprint through scripture in 90 days — well suited to a season of fasting, a sabbatical, or a small group doing it together.

Each challenge starts wherever you begin reading and runs day by day from there — there's no wrong day to start.
